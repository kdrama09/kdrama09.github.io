SD Solid Drama — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SD Solid Drama handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We May Process

SD Solid Drama may process information needed to operate the app, including your Pi account or wallet-related information when required for Pi Network features, app activity needed to provide streaming and reward functions, and technical information necessary for security and reliability.

2. SDToken Rewards

The app may provide SDToken rewards based on eligible viewing activity and a cooldown-based reward system. Reward records may be associated with your app account or Pi identity as needed to operate the service and prevent abuse.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To provide and maintain SD Solid Drama.
  • To authenticate users and support Pi Network functionality.
  • To calculate eligible rewards and enforce cooldown rules.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, or manipulation of the reward system.
  • To improve security, performance, and user experience.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or network partners when reasonably necessary to operate the app, comply with applicable law, protect users, or maintain security.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and reward records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

SD Solid Drama is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use Pi Network or the applicable service.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the SD Solid Drama team through the contact method provided in the app or its official project channels.
